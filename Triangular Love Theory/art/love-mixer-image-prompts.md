# Love Mixer — Art Prompts

Eight images for the Love Mixer embed, one per love type. Written for ChatGPT image
generation.

Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/D9EJh6Q9qTem4pyF5JyLMZ

## Read me first

This is the third version of this sheet, and the two earlier failures are worth knowing
about because they pull in opposite directions.

**Version one** asked for dense, ornate Mary Blair paintings with decorative borders. They
were beautiful and completely illegible at the ~200px the page shows them.

**Version two** over-corrected. It said "extremely simple," "two figures and at most three
symbols," "no small fiddly detail anywhere" — and got flat, generic mannequins that looked
like 2005 Flash animation. That was the wrong lesson. **Reading well at small size comes
from clear composition, not from a bare drawing.** The Dr. Love's Office pictures are full
of detail — striped hoodies, dungarees, potted plants, wall clocks — and they read
perfectly at the same size, because each one has a single obvious thing happening in the
middle of the frame.

So this version does three things differently:

1. **It uses the Dr. Love's Office style brief almost verbatim**, in the Mixer's palette.
   That brief is proven; the "keep it simple" one is not.
2. **Every image names its characters** — hair, build, face — the way the Dr. Love couple
   prompts do. Unnamed "two figures" is what produced the mannequins.
3. **Every scene is now a joke**, not a diagram. A couple ignoring each other in bed while a
   tumbleweed rolls past is funny; two people standing beside a ring is a pictogram.

Faces and bodies should be **comically exaggerated** — big noses, wild hair, gangly limbs,
double chins, enormous eyes. Dry and deadpan, never cute.

## The style brief

Send this once, first, in the chat you will generate all eight images in.

> I need a set of eight cartoon illustrations that all look like they came from the same
> sketchbook. Here is the style, and it applies to every image I ask for in this
> conversation.
>
> Spare, graphic cartoon drawing. Everything built from thick, confident black ink contours
> of even weight, like a felt-tip pen. Flat areas of colour inside the lines, no shading, no
> gradients, no photographic texture. Figures are angular and simplified but full of
> character, with oversized round eyes, tiny dot pupils and deadpan, under-reacting
> expressions. **Exaggerate faces and bodies for comedy** — big noses, wild hair, gangly or
> lumpy builds, expressive hands. Dry and funny rather than cute.
>
> These are shown small, so every picture needs **one clear thing happening in the middle of
> the frame**, reading instantly as a silhouette. Detail is welcome — props, clothing
> patterns, furniture — as long as it supports that one action rather than competing with
> it. No decorative borders or patterned frames.
>
> Use exactly these six colours and nothing else:
> — warm bone white #F2E5CD for the background and most interior shapes
> — deep near-black #17121F for every outline and all linework
> — hot vermilion red #E0462F
> — muted teal-cyan #4A9FB0
> — dark ochre gold #D4972E
> — olive green #7F8C3C
>
> Across the set I will ask you to draw several different couples. **They must look like
> different people** — vary hair, face shape, body shape, age and height every time, and make
> the two people within each couple obviously different from each other.
>
> Every image: square, flat 2D cartoon, plain bone-white background, no signature, no
> watermark, no border drawn around the picture. No text or lettering unless I specifically
> ask for it.
>
> Please confirm you have this, then I will send the images one at a time.

## The eight images

One per message, after the style brief.

**1. `non-love.jpg`** — the dead-eyed scroll

> A couple sitting up in a double bed, side by side, each staring down at a phone, faces lit
> by the screens, completely blank and bored. Neither acknowledges the other. He is scrawny
> with enormous bed-hair sticking straight up and a slack open mouth; she is round-faced with
> a green clay face mask and hair curlers. A dusty tumbleweed is blowing across the duvet in
> the gap between them. A cobweb in the corner of the headboard.

**2. `infatuation.jpg`** — the chaos under the covers

> A double bed seen from the side, the duvet heaped and billowing in a wild chaotic mound
> with motion lines and a small dust cloud coming off it. You cannot see the couple at all —
> only four bare feet sticking out from under the covers at completely absurd angles, one
> foot pointing straight up. A bedside lamp knocked crooked, its shade askew. A startled cat
> leaping off the end of the bed.

**3. `liking.jpg`** — the sleepover

> Two friends sitting cross-legged facing each other on a couch in pyjamas, each braiding the
> other's hair at the same time, both mid-sentence and delighted, mouths wide open talking
> over each other. One has an enormous round afro being wrestled into a braid; the other has
> very long straight hair down to her waist. A bowl of popcorn tipped over between them. No
> hearts — this is friendship, not romance.

**4. `empty-love.jpg`** — trapped in the ring

> A gigantic ochre wedding ring clamped around the waists of a husband and wife, pinning them
> back to back inside it like a barrel. Both strain outward in opposite directions, arms
> stretched, feet braced and sliding, faces set in grim resignation rather than panic. He is
> tall and droopy with a walrus moustache; she is short and square with a tight grey perm.
> They have clearly been doing this for years.

**5. `romantic-love.jpg`** — the balcony

> Romeo and Juliet. Juliet leans over a small stone balcony at the top right, chin in both
> hands, gazing down with enormous adoring eyes, long dark hair spilling over the rail. Romeo
> stands below at the bottom left in a puffy-sleeved doublet and tights, one hand clasped to
> his chest, the other flung out mid-declaration, with a speech bubble full of red hearts
> coming out of his mouth. A crescent moon. He has a terrible bowl haircut.

**6. `fatuous-love.jpg`** — straight down the aisle

> A bride and groom sprinting hand in hand down a church aisle toward the viewer, veil and
> coat-tails streaming behind, both grinning enormously with wide manic eyes, not looking at
> each other. Speed lines and a shocked guest's hat flying off. Between their heads is a
> single shared thought bubble containing the words "HAPPILY EVER AFTER" in neat hand-lettered
> capitals. He is beefy with a jaw like a shovel; she is tiny and birdlike.
>
> **This is the only image with words in it.** Check the spelling carefully — image models
> garble text. If it comes out wrong after two tries, ask for the thought bubble empty and
> tell me; I'll lay the words over it in the page instead.

**7. `companionate-love.jpg`** — the serene grandparents

> A living room in uproar. In the front of the frame, large and central, an elderly couple sit
> side by side on a couch holding hands, utterly serene, faint contented smiles, completely
> unbothered. Behind and around them, smaller: their two grown-up children standing with
> hands on heads in horror, and three small grandchildren wrecking the place — one swinging
> from a curtain, one drawing on the wall, one upending a plant pot. The old couple are calm
> and still; everything behind them is motion lines and chaos. Keep the couple clearly the
> biggest thing in the picture.

**8. `consummate-love.jpg`** — extremely pleased with themselves

> An older couple lying side by side in bed under a ruffled duvet, both looking enormously
> satisfied. Their four bare feet stick out at the bottom of the covers. She has a tangle of
> grey hair, is propped on one elbow and is smoking a cigarette with a thin curl of smoke
> rising. He lies back with his hands behind his head, giving her a sly wink and a crooked
> half-smile. A knocked-over glass of water on the nightstand.

### If something gets refused

Two of these touch things image models are sometimes squeamish about. Nothing explicit is
ever visible in either, so a plain re-ask usually works, but if not:

- **Image 2 or 8 refused as suggestive** — say "a comedic cartoon, nobody is visible, this is
  a joke about a messy bed," or reframe image 2 as a chaotic pillow fight.
- **The cigarette in image 8 refused** — swap it for her blowing a large pink bubble with
  bubblegum, which lands much the same joke.

above does not really touch it.

## What not to replace

**The jars, ingredients, pot, spoon and hand stay as SVG.** They are drawn inside the page,
not as image files, because they animate — lids tilt open, the hand reaches in and carries
two ingredients across, the spoon stirs, the pot tips and pours. A flat picture cannot do any
of that.

**Format and size.** Square, around 800×800, saved as JPEG. ChatGPT returns PNGs of roughly
1.4 MB each; re-saved as JPEG at quality 88 the same picture is around 200 KB with no
visible difference at display size. The page looks for `.jpg` first and `.png` second, so
either works — drop the PNGs straight in if that's easier and I'll convert them.

**The names are exact** — the page builds the path from the love type, so a typo means that
one type silently falls back to its drawn placeholder. You can replace them one at a time;
nothing breaks while the set is half old and half new.
